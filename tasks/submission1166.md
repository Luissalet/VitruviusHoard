# Retained managed graph selection

`managed.graph_retained` projects one to three selected historical observations: at most one each of `managed.artifact`, `managed.members` and `managed.native_boundaries`. The original `managed.graph` from the binary remains available. The incorporated projection helper is unchanged; this path supplies selected Evidence rather than rerunning readers against the assembly.

Every input document is reread and checked against its receipt hash and operation/artifact bindings. The selected normalized artifact SHA is checked separately; maintained schemas enforce token scopes and module identities. Contradictions fail, missing module identity remains unknown, and two observations of the same facet are rejected rather than merged. Valid documents in the declared terminal states, including imported, may be selected. Project and first-artifact admission remains required.

Historical carriers identify the current retained projection provider, with derived confidence and historical authority. Their authenticated parameters and raw result bind the exact source JSON bytes and SHA. Source observations retain all occurrence IDs independently of carrier identity, including supplied closure. Missing closure is diagnosed; an unselected closure facet is never projected. This proves retained integrity, not the truth or completeness of the historical extractor.

New `managed.members` documents record their effective type/method window and string selection in `observation_scope`, inside the hash-bound JSON. Older documents without that declaration have unknown window scope. The graph envelope keeps source coverage, observation selection and the helper's coverage distinct. `complete-within-inputs` permits omitted facets and does not establish a complete program inventory. Graph locations are historical coordinates, not current filesystem checks. The projection includes containment and native boundaries; it does not recover a CIL call graph or execute managed code.

Occurrence provider context is retained separately from carrier identity. Compilation and TypeScript checking passed again.
